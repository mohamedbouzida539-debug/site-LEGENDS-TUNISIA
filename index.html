Server Rules

Welcome to the server! To ensure this place remains a safe, respectful, and fun environment for everyone, please read and strictly follow the rules below.

🚫

Strictly No Blasphemy or Religious Slurs

Respecting beliefs is a red line. Cursing or insulting God or religions in any form is strictly prohibited. Violating this rule will result in an immediate ban.

Instant Ban
🚫

No Cursing Mothers or Families

Members' families are strictly off-limits, even as a joke. Cursing mothers or insulting anyone's family is completely forbidden. Conversations must remain respectful at all times.

High
🚫

No Foul Language (Especially Around Girls)

We aim to build a respectful community; therefore, the use of profanity and inappropriate words is banned. We strongly emphasize maintaining utmost respect and politeness, especially in channels where girls are present.

Medium
🚫

No Server Advertising or DM Promoting

Advertising or promoting other Discord servers within this server is strictly forbidden. Sending server invite links in DMs to members you interact with here is also not allowed.

Medium
🚫

No NSFW, Gore, or Disturbing Content in DMs

Sending inappropriate, explicit/NSFW content, or violent and disturbing material (such as crimes, murder, or gore) to any member of this server via DMs is strictly prohibited.

High
💡 Important Note: The rules apply to absolutely everyone. Even if it is my own brother, I will not forgive or make exceptions!
